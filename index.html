A NAME FAMILIES TRUST
IN FERTILITY CARE

Dr. Geetha Haripriya is a senior gynecologist and
IVF specialist in Chennai with over 40 years of experience
in reproductive medicine. She is the Chairperson of
Prashanth Fertility and Prashanth Multispeciality Hospital.

She has helped more than 45,000 couples through fertility
treatments including IVF, IUI, and ICSI, and has been part
of over 22,000 to 50,000 successful births through assisted
reproductive techniques. She has also performed over
40,000 laparoscopic and hysteroscopic surgeries.

Widely respected in India and internationally,
Dr. Geetha Haripriya is known for her expertise,
experience and compassionate approach to fertility care.
